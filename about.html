About Keep Quiet

By Natalia Jakubczyk-Gajewska · Last updated: November 13, 2026

Keep Quiet (keepquiet.app) is a website with free classroom tools that run in the browser, and the home of the Noise Meter – Keep Quiet app for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Who makes it

Keep Quiet is made by Natalia Jakubczyk-Gajewska (Apps for Parents), the developer of both the website and the app.

All apps by Natalia Jakubczyk-Gajewska on the App Store

The website: free classroom tools

The first tool is the free online classroom noise meter on the homepage. Friendly characters react when the room gets louder, so kids lower their own volume. Loudness is shown as a character, a scene colour and a green-to-red dial or bar.

  • Free, no ads, no sign-up, no time limit.
  • Works in the browser on Chromebook, Windows, Mac and iPad. It needs a microphone and a current browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
  • Privacy: Audio never leaves your device — sound is analysed in the browser, nothing is recorded, stored or sent.

An optional one-time unlock adds all 8 characters and all 5 backgrounds to the online noise meter. Everything in the free meter stays free. The price and the details are in the pricing section.

The app: Noise Meter – Keep Quiet

Noise Meter – Keep Quiet is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that shows a room's noise level visually: friendly characters react in real time as the room gets louder. It is made for classrooms, home and sensory-friendly spaces. There is no Android version.

  • Free to download, no ads. Optional premium characters and themes are available via in-app purchase; plans and prices are shown in the app.
  • Privacy: the microphone is used only to measure the volume level in real time — the app does not record audio.
