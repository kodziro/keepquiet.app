Privacy Policy – keepquiet.app

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what keepquiet.app does with your data. The short version: the free noise meter collects nothing personal, and for a purchase we store only what is needed to deliver and restore your license.

  • Who we are. Apps for Parents, Natalia Jakubczyk-Gajewska, Poland. Contact: support@keepquiet.app. This page covers the website and the browser unlock. The Noise Meter – Keep Quiet app for iPhone, iPad and Mac has its own privacy policy.
  • The noise meter. Audio never leaves your device — sound is analysed in the browser, nothing is recorded, stored or sent. No account, no cookies, no ads.
  • Anonymous usage statistics. We use TelemetryDeck to count how the site is used. The data is anonymous and aggregated: no personal data and no tracking across sites. It tells us how often the tool is started, how long sessions last, which settings are used, how often people come back and whether the unlock is used.
  • Buying the unlock. You pay on Stripe's page; we never see your card details. Link (Stripe) is the seller and has its own privacy policy. From Stripe we receive and store the email address you paid with, your license key and the payment id. We use them to deliver, restore and support your purchase and to handle refunds. They are stored on Cloudflare, which hosts our unlock service; the key email is sent through Resend. Our providers may process data outside the EU under EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We keep them for the life of the license. On request we delete your email address — after that you can still restore with your license key or restore link, but not by email. Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract) for the purchase data, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest) for abuse checks and rate limiting.
  • In your browser. The unlock keeps your license key and a random device id in your browser's localStorage. They are needed for the unlock to work. The random id only lets us count the devices per key (an abuse check); it identifies no person, and we delete each device record 30 days after it was last seen. Clearing your site data removes them; your restore link brings the key back. The free noise meter also saves your settings (microphone, indicator, sensitivity), a visit count with the date of your first visit, your feedback vote and whether you saw the fullscreen hint — all in your browser only, nothing personal.
  • Rate limiting. Cloudflare processes your IP address to protect the service from too many requests. We do not store it.
  • Email. When you write to us, we use your message only to answer you. We do not sell or share personal data.
  • Your rights. You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, or object to its use — write to the contact address above. You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority (PUODO).
